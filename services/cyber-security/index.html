FAQ

Cyber security: common questions

What is Cyber Essentials?

Cyber Essentials is a UK government-backed certification that shows you have five basic security controls in place. Cyber Essentials Plus adds an independent technical check. Read our plain-English guide to Cyber Essentials.

Is multi-factor authentication really necessary?

Yes. Stolen or guessed passwords are one of the most common ways attackers get in, and multi-factor authentication stops most of those attacks. It is also required for cloud services under Cyber Essentials.

What should a small business fix first?

Usually: turn on multi-factor authentication everywhere, keep devices and software updated, make sure backups work and are kept separate, stop using admin accounts for everyday work, and help staff spot phishing emails.

How often should we review our security?

At least once a year, and whenever something significant changes, such as a new system, a new site or a move to the cloud. Cyber Essentials certification is also renewed every year.
