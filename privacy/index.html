Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal information. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who we are

This website (btree.uk, including the ShiftFlow pages) is run by BTree Technologies Limited, trading as BTree, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 16621870. We are based in Bradford, United Kingdom.

We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice. If you have any questions about it, email us at info@btree.uk.

What we collect

When you send us an enquiry through the contact form, we collect your name, email address and message, and, if you choose to give them, your company name and phone number. We also record which service you asked about, the page you sent the form from, the date and time, and the country your request came from (worked out by Cloudflare from your connection). We do not store your IP address with your enquiry.

When you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything else you choose to include.

When you visit the site, our hosting and security providers process technical information such as your IP address and browser type so they can deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse. We use cookies for analytics only if you agree to them; see Cookies and analytics below. Your choice is remembered in your browser on your device.

How we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, to give you a quote, demo or proposal. This is in our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us, or is a step you have asked us to take before entering into a contract.
  • To keep the contact form free from spam and abuse. This is in our legitimate interests in keeping our services secure.
  • To keep a record of our communications with you. This is in our legitimate interests in running our business.
  • To understand how people use the site so we can improve it (Google Analytics). We do this only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We will not add you to a marketing list or send you newsletters unless you ask us to, and we never sell your information.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers who process information on our behalf, under contracts that require them to keep it secure:

  • Cloudflare, which runs our contact form service and stores enquiries, and provides Turnstile, the check that stops automated spam.
  • Microsoft, which provides our email (Microsoft 365). Enquiries are sent to our team by email.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Google, which provides Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

International transfers

Some of these providers process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by UK adequacy regulations (such as the UK-US data bridge) or by the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum approved by the Information Commissioner.

Cookies and analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics (loaded through Google Tag Manager) to see how many people visit, which pages they read, how they found us and whether they send an enquiry. Google Analytics does not record or store IP addresses, and we do not use it for advertising. Google Analytics keeps this information for 14 months. If you do not accept, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics are not loaded at all.

You can change your mind at any time with , also at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, we stop analytics and remove its cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries sent through the contact form for 24 months, after which they are deleted automatically. If you become a customer, we keep the information we need for our working relationship for as long as that relationship lasts and for up to 6 years afterwards, to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to us using it;
  • ask us to transfer it to you or to another organisation.

To use any of these rights, email info@btree.uk. We will reply within one month. There is normally no charge.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

ShiftFlow app users

This notice covers this website and enquiries sent to us. If your employer uses ShiftFlow, your employer is responsible for the staff information held in it, and its own privacy notice explains how that information is used.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
