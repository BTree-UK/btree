Kiosk

A time clock on any tablet

No proprietary hardware. Install the kiosk on an iPad or Android tablet, lock it to the app, and your team clocks in with their PIN.

  1. Pair the tabletEnter a one-time pairing code and the tablet is tied to that venue.
  2. Tap your name, enter your PINClock in, start a break, clock out. Pick the position if you hold more than one.
  3. Works offlinePunches are kept on the device and sent the moment the connection returns.
  4. See it liveThe dashboard shows who is on shift now, and the timesheet fills itself.

Security and platform

Serious about your people's data

Staff records hold passports, bank details and National Insurance numbers. ShiftFlow was built to protect them from the first line of code.

Encrypted at rest

Sensitive fields and documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with managed key rotation.

Tenant isolation

Every business is separated at the database itself with row level security, not only in the application.

SSO and MFA

Single sign-on with Microsoft Entra ID or any OIDC provider, plus authenticator app MFA.

Granular permissions

Roles built from fine-grained permissions, scoped to the locations each manager looks after.

Full audit trail

Every change is recorded with who, when and what it was before, ready for any dispute.

Settings that inherit

Set it once for the business, override it for a single venue only where you need to.
