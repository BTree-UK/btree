FAQ

ShiftFlow for manufacturing: common questions

Can ShiftFlow handle 12-hour shift patterns?

Yes. Shift lengths and patterns are up to you, and rest between shifts is still checked.

Can we see overtime before it happens?

Yes. Hours and cost update as you build the rota, and reports show attendance and overtime against what was planned.

Does it handle night shifts?

Yes. Shifts can run past midnight and stay on the day they started, and night work limits for under-18s are checked.
