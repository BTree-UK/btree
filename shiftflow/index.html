Kiosk

A time clock on any tablet

No proprietary hardware. Install the kiosk on an iPad or Android tablet, lock it to the app, and your team clocks in with their PIN.

  1. Pair the tabletEnter a one-time pairing code and the tablet is tied to that site.
  2. Tap your name, enter your PINClock in, start a break, clock out. Pick the position if you hold more than one.
  3. Works offlinePunches are kept on the device and sent the moment the connection returns.
  4. See it liveThe dashboard shows who is on shift now, and the timesheet fills itself.

FAQ

ShiftFlow: common questions

What is ShiftFlow?

ShiftFlow is a workforce platform for UK businesses that run on shifts. It brings rotas, clock-ins, timesheets, holiday, documents and HR records together in one place, so the hours on the rota, the punches at the door and the figures sent to payroll always agree.

Does ShiftFlow check UK working time rules?

Yes. Every rota is checked before it is published, including 11 hours rest between shifts, the 48-hour average week, minimum breaks, limits for under-18s and visa weekly hour caps. Errors block publishing and warnings are shown, and each rule can be tuned for each site.

What devices does ShiftFlow work on?

Managers use ShiftFlow in a web browser. Staff clock in on an iPad or Android tablet running the kiosk app, and use the employee app on iOS or Android to see shifts, book holiday and swap shifts.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The kiosk keeps working. Clock-ins are stored on the tablet and sent as soon as the connection returns.

Can ShiftFlow handle several sites?

Yes. Staff can have a home site and other sites they work at, double bookings across sites are blocked, open shifts can be offered across sites, and settings can be set once for the business and changed for each site.

Does ShiftFlow work with our payroll?

Approved hours go into a pay run export that you can give to your payroll provider or import into your payroll software.

Can staff sign in with their Microsoft account?

Yes. ShiftFlow supports single sign-on with Microsoft Entra ID or any OIDC provider, as well as authenticator app multi-factor authentication.

How much does ShiftFlow cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your team and the parts you use. Book a demo and we will show you ShiftFlow and give you a quote.
